# Official sources

Use this reference only when you need source grounding for Dynamics 365 Customer Service behavior or the case-to-resolution business process.

## Microsoft Learn documentation

Use these as starting points, not as proof of the user's live Customer Service configuration or operational metrics:

- https://learn.microsoft.com/dynamics365/customer-service/implement/overview — Customer Service capabilities: cases, interactions, knowledge base, unified routing, omnichannel (incl. voice), SLAs, entitlements, dashboards. Administration via the Copilot Service admin center. Supports scope and KPI workflow steps.
- https://learn.microsoft.com/dynamics365/customer-service/administer/overview-cases — Case management components: cases, activities, entitlements, knowledge articles, queues, Copilot case summaries, SLAs, record creation/update rules, routing rules. Supports the case-management review step.
- https://learn.microsoft.com/dynamics365/customer-service/administer/create-enhanced-sla — Enhanced SLAs: SLA KPIs, applicable-when conditions, success criteria, warning/failure actions. Supports the SLA review step.
- https://learn.microsoft.com/dynamics365/customer-service/administer/create-standard-sla — Standard SLAs and the available SLA workflow actions. Supports the SLA design and pushback steps.
- https://learn.microsoft.com/dynamics365/guidance/business-processes/case-to-resolution-overview — Case to resolution end-to-end process and featured capabilities: Omnichannel, unified routing, knowledge base, Microsoft Teams collaboration, AI suggestions, Customer Insights, Customer Voice. Supports the end-to-end lifecycle workflow.

## Certification alignment (verify currency)

- Exam MB-230: Microsoft Dynamics 365 Customer Service Functional Consultant — skills measured updated as of March 11, 2026 (verify status before citing): case management (51–55%), representative experience and routing (25–30%), extend Customer Service (15–20%).

## Terminology note

The Customer Service admin center was renamed the Copilot Service admin center. Some Learn articles may use either name. Verify current naming before presenting configuration locations.

## Grounding rule

Official documentation explains Customer Service behavior. It does not prove the user's actual SLA attainment, routing accuracy, knowledge freshness, channel configuration, or CSAT. Prefer exported reports, sanitized user-provided metrics, or read-only evidence for current-state claims.
