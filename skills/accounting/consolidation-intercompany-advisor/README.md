# Consolidation & Intercompany Advisor Skill

Multi-jurisdiction reference framework for consolidation scope determination and intercompany elimination advisory across 6 major accounting frameworks.

## Coverage

- **Consolidation scope**: ASC 810 two-tier model (voting interest entities + VIEs) vs. IFRS 10 three-element control model; VIE primary beneficiary test (power + expected losses/benefits); IFRS 10 de-facto control (B41-B46) and substantive potential voting rights (B38-B50)
- **Local GAAP consolidation**: German HGB § 290-315a (Konzernabschluss size criteria and sub-group exemption); JGAAP ASBJ Statement No. 22; CAS 33 (China, including VIE structures for offshore-listed groups); Ind AS 110 (word-for-word IFRS 10)
- **NCI measurement**: Fair value method (US GAAP mandatory; IFRS 3.B44 choice) vs. proportionate share method; subsequent NCI accounting including deficit NCI and transactions with NCI without loss of control
- **Equity method**: ASC 323 / IAS 28 significant influence (20% presumption); upstream and downstream intercompany profit elimination; losses in excess of investment; impairment of equity method investments
- **Intercompany eliminations**: Intercompany sales and COGS; profit-in-inventory; intercompany loans, interest, and dividends; full elimination principle (100% regardless of NCI percentage); deferred tax on IC eliminations (ASC 740-10 / IAS 12.39) at buyer's rate
- **Transfer pricing**: Arm's length vs. cost-plus; versioned elimination rules approach; retroactive vs. prospective treatment of price changes
- **Cross-border constraints**: SAFE intercompany loan registration requirements; CSRC A-share group reporting; EU Transparency Directive consolidated filing deadlines
- **Adversarial scenarios**: M&A mid-quarter-close quarantine workflow; cross-border IC matching break investigation; transfer price change affecting eliminations; investment entity exception (ASC 946 / IFRS 10.27-33)
