# Lease Accounting Advisor Skill

Multi-jurisdiction reference framework for lease accounting advisory across 7 major jurisdictions and standards.

## Coverage

- Lease identification: identified asset test, supplier substitution right, lease vs. service distinction (ASC 842-10-15-9; IFRS 16.9)
- Lessee classification: ASC 842 dual model (finance / operating) vs. IFRS 16 single model; five finance lease criteria (transfer of ownership, purchase option, major part of economic life, substantially all fair value, specialized nature)
- ROU asset and lease liability: initial and subsequent measurement; payment components included vs. excluded
- Discount rates: rate implicit in lease vs. incremental borrowing rate (IBR); IBR determination factors (credit quality, term, collateral, currency, economic environment); non-PBE risk-free rate practical expedient (ASC 842)
- Lessor accounting: sales-type / direct-financing / operating classification and income recognition patterns
- Short-term and low-value exemptions: ASC 842 ≤ 12-month class-level election; IFRS 16 ≤ 12-month and low-value (≈$5K) lease-level elections; CAS 21 low-value ≈ CNY 40K
- Lease modifications and remeasurement: separate new lease test; modification of existing lease; partial termination gain/loss
- Sale-leaseback: sale test under ASC 606 / IFRS 15; seller-lessee and buyer-lessor accounting; failed sale-leaseback financing treatment
- Multi-jurisdiction effective dates: UK FRS 102 amendments effective 1 Jan 2026; JGAAP ASBJ Statement No. 34 effective FY beginning on/after 1 Apr 2027
- GAAP comparison table: ASC 842, IFRS 16, UK FRS 102, German HGB, JGAAP (pre/post Statement No. 34), CAS 21, Ind AS 116
